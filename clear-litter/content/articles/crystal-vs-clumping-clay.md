The two most common litters on the shelf couldn't be more different. Here's a
side-by-side look to help you choose.

## Quick comparison

| Factor | Crystal (silica) | Clumping clay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Odor control | Excellent | Good |
| Tracking | Very low | Moderate to high |
| Dust | Very low | Varies |
| Scooping | Solids only | Solids + urine clumps |
| Change frequency | ~3–4 weeks (1 cat) | ~1–2 weeks |
| Upfront cost | Higher | Lower |
| Flushable | No | No |

## Where crystal wins

If your top priorities are **odor**, **low tracking**, and **less frequent
cleaning**, crystal is hard to beat. It's also the lighter bag to carry home.

## Where clumping clay wins

Clay is **cheaper per pound**, **widely available**, and its clumps make it
obvious what to scoop — which matters a lot in **multi-cat homes**. Many cats
also simply prefer the finer, sand-like texture.

## Our recommendation

- **One cat, want convenience?** Try crystal.
- **Multiple cats or tight budget?** A quality clumping clay is still a great
  workhorse.
- **Sensitive to dust?** Look at low-dust crystal or a plant-based clumping
  option.

Whichever you pick, transition over 7–10 days by mixing the new litter into the
old so your cat accepts the change.
