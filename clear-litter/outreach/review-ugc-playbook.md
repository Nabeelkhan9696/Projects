# Reviews & UGC Playbook

Goal: 10–15 photo/story reviews concentrated on **Health Monitoring** and
**Fresh Scent**, plus a homepage UGC gallery. Platform: **Judge.me only**
(already installed). No Trustpilot/Yelp — revisit Trustpilot at meaningful
order volume.

## One-time Judge.me setup (app admin — ~15 min)

1. **Product grouping:** Judge.me → Settings → Product groups. Create group
   "Crystal Litter (scented)" containing Fresh Scent, Lavender, Citrus
   Breeze, and the Variety Pack so they pool reviews. Keep **Health
   Monitoring separate** — its reviews must speak to the health claims.
2. **Review request email:** enable automatic request ~14 days after
   fulfillment (bag has been in use ~2 weeks — real opinions, before the
   refill window). One reminder after 7 days, then stop.
3. **Photo incentive:** offer a coupon for photo reviews (e.g. 15% next
   order). Text reviews get a thank-you, photos get the coupon.
4. **Google:** once any product/group reaches ~5 reviews, connect the
   Judge.me product-ratings feed to Google Merchant Center (Judge.me →
   Settings → Integrations → Google Shopping) for stars on free listings.

## Sampling program (new reviews for Health Monitoring)

Offer a free 8 lb bag of Health Monitoring litter in exchange for an
**honest** review. Target owners of senior (7+), male, or UTI-history cats
— they're the ones who can produce the "it caught something early" story
review, which is the single most valuable content we can own.

Rules (keep us clean): recipient must disclose they received a free
product; never condition the coupon/free bag on a *positive* review; 4-star
honest beats 5-star generic.

## UGC pipeline

The homepage now has a **"Loved by cats. And their people."** gallery
(staging theme). It renders only when photos are added — invisible until
then.

**To add content:** Online Store → Themes → *Clear Litter — Staging (Claude
edits here)* → Customize → select the homepage section → **Add block → UGC
photo** → upload image, caption (`"Biscuit, 7 — @catmomjules"`), optional
link to a product or the original post. Up to 12. Portrait 4:5, ≥800 px
wide works best. Publish staging when it looks right.

**Rights:** before reposting a customer's photo/video, get a yes in
writing (DM reply is fine): "Love this — can we feature it on our site and
social with credit to @handle?" Keep a screenshot of the consent.

**What to collect, in priority order:**
1. Health Monitoring color-change photos in a real box (with the story)
2. Cat-using-the-box lifestyle shots (any scent)
3. "One bag vs four clay bags" comparison shots
4. Short clips: pouring (no dust), scooping routine — future ad creative
