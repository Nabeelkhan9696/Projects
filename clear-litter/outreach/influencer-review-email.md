# Influencer / Creator Review Outreach

**Goal:** honest product reviews from cat-content creators — YouTube, TikTok,
Instagram, and cat blogs. Each review earns: a backlink (blogs), social proof
to embed on product pages (with permission), and buyer-intent traffic.

**Who to pitch:** micro-creators, roughly 3k–80k followers. They respond,
their audiences trust them, and free product is fair compensation at that
size. One good hook: creators whose cats have had urinary issues — the
health-monitoring litter is a story, not an ad.

**Rules:**
- Free product, no payment, and explicitly **no script** — honest opinion
  only, including criticism. (Honest reviews convert better and keep you
  clean with FTC disclosure rules; they must disclose the free product.)
- Never ask for a positive review. Ask for a *fair* one.
- Follow up once after ~7 days, then stop.
