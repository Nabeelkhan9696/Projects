# Shelter / Rescue Donation Outreach

**Goal:** donate litter to local shelters and rescues. The donation is genuinely
good; the SEO benefit is the "Our Sponsors" / "Thank You" backlink from a
trusted `.org` domain, plus local press pickup and social mentions.

**Targets:** cat rescues and municipal shelters within driving distance of
Orange, CA first (LA/OC has dozens), then anywhere willing to post about it.
Prioritize orgs with an active website and social accounts.

**Ask:** nothing up front. Offer the donation unconditionally; *after* they
accept, mention that a link or social tag helps you donate to more shelters.
Never make the link a condition — it reads as (and is) link-buying.

---

## Email template

**Subject:** Free crystal cat litter for [Shelter Name]'s cats

Hi [Name],

I'm Nabeel, founder of Clear Litter — we make silica crystal cat litter and
we're based in Orange, CA. I'd like to donate a case of our crystal litter
([X] × 8 lb bags) to [Shelter Name], no strings attached.

Crystal litter tends to work well in shelter settings: it's 99% dust-free
(easier on cats with respiratory issues), controls ammonia odor for weeks,
and the bags are light enough for volunteers to handle easily.

If that would be useful, just reply with a delivery address and I'll ship it
this week. And if it works well for your cats, we're happy to make it a
recurring monthly donation.

Thanks for everything you do for these cats,

Nabeel Khan
Founder, Clear Litter
www.clearlitter.com · info@clearlitter.com
